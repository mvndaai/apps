Split Play

Control two audio apps at once on Samsung Galaxy phones.

Samsung's Separate app sound feature lets one app play through the phone's speaker while every other app plays through your Bluetooth headphones or speaker. Split Play shows both outputs side by side, so you can control each one without switching apps.

Features

  • A card for each output, Bluetooth and phone speaker, showing the app, title, and artwork that's playing there.
  • Play, pause, skip, jump 30 seconds back or forward, and seek on each card.
  • A separate volume slider for each output.
  • Choose which card your phone's volume and media buttons control.
  • Pick the Bluetooth device from the system output switcher.
  • An option to keep the phone unlocked while the app is open. The screen still dims.
  • Custom colors for each card, and a side-by-side layout in landscape.

Requirements

  • A Samsung Galaxy phone with One UI and Android 16 or later.
  • Separate app sound, which you turn on in Settings > Sounds and vibration > Separate app sound.
  • Notification access for Split Play. Android requires this permission before an app can see and control other apps' media playback.

Get started

  1. Open Split Play and tap Grant Permission, then turn on notification access for Split Play.
  2. Turn on Separate app sound and choose the app that plays on the phone speaker. Split Play's settings button opens that screen for you.
  3. Start playing audio in two apps. Each one appears on its own card.

To see how the app works before you set it up, tap Demo on the permission card.
