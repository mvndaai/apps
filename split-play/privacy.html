Split Play privacy policy

Effective November 16, 2026

This policy explains what information the Split Play Android app uses and what happens to it. In short, what Split Play reads about your media and devices stays on your phone. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, and the ad service collects some device information, as described in Ads.

Information the app uses

To work, Split Play reads the following information on your phone. It uses this information only while it runs, to show it on screen and to control playback.

  • Media playback from other apps. With the notification access you grant, Split Play reads the media sessions of apps that are playing audio: the app name, title, artist, album, artwork, playback position, and whether it's playing. Split Play uses this to show each app on its card and to send play, pause, skip, and seek commands to it. Split Play doesn't read the content of your notifications.
  • Audio devices and volume. Split Play reads which audio outputs are connected, and their volume levels, so it can show and change the volume of each output.
  • Bluetooth device names. With the Nearby devices permission (BLUETOOTH_CONNECT), Split Play reads the name of the connected Bluetooth headphones or speaker to show it on the card. If you don't grant this permission, the app shows a general name instead.
  • Separate app sound settings. Split Play reads whether Samsung's Separate app sound is on, which app it's set to, and which output that app uses, so it can put each app on the right card.

Information the app saves

Split Play saves only your choices in the app: whether to keep the phone unlocked, the color and order of the cards, the display size, whether the device control button is shown, and whether you bought ad removal. These choices are stored on your phone. If you use Android backup, Android might include them in your device backup to your Google account, as it does for other apps.

Ads

Split Play shows a banner ad in portrait using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob). To choose and show ads, measure them, and prevent fraud, the SDK collects information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Google processes this information under the Google Privacy Policy. To learn more, see how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

To reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads, go to Settings > Google > All services > Ads on your phone.

Purchases

You can remove ads with an in-app purchase. Google Play handles the purchase and your payment information. Split Play receives only whether you bought ad removal, so it can hide the ads.

Sharing

Split Play doesn't send your media, playback, audio device, Bluetooth, or Separate app sound information off your phone, and it contains no analytics code. The developer doesn't receive any of your information. The only information that leaves your phone is what the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects to show ads, and what Google Play uses to process a purchase.

Permissions

  • Notification access: to see and control other apps' media playback.
  • Nearby devices (BLUETOOTH_CONNECT): to show the connected Bluetooth device's name.
  • Change audio settings (MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS): to change the volume of each output.
  • Internet and network access (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): to load ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK adds these permissions.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID): to show and measure ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK adds this permission.
  • Google Play billing (BILLING): to let you buy ad removal.

You can turn off notification access or the Nearby devices permission at any time in your phone's settings.

Children

Split Play isn't directed at children. It doesn't knowingly collect personal information from children, and it doesn't collect any personal information itself.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new effective date.
